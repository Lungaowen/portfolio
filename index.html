How I think

The architecture starts with the problem.

I am less interested in adding technology for its own sake and more interested in making the right trade-offs for the product.

01

Start with the problem

Understand the users, constraints, business model and real-world workflow before choosing frameworks or infrastructure.

02

Design the boundaries

Separate responsibilities around clear domains, contracts and data ownership so the system can change without everything changing with it.

03

Build for change

Good architecture should make the next decision easier — whether that means adding a new tenant, service, integration, region or product capability.
